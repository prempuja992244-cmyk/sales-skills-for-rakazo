# Competitor battlecard

## Research (public sources only)
Competitor website, pricing page, reviews (G2, Capterra, Google reviews, Justdial, app stores), social posts, job posts, and what our prospects/customers have said about them in CRM notes.

## Battlecard format (1 page per competitor)
- **Who they are**: positioning in one line, typical customer, price range.
- **Where they're strong**: be honest — 3 points.
- **Where we win**: 3 points backed by proof.
- **Where we lose**: when we shouldn't compete.
- **Discovery questions that expose gaps** ("How do you handle {thing we do well} today?").
- **Answers to "Why not just use them?"** — 2–3 sentence replies.
- **Their likely attacks on us** and our calm responses.
- **Proof**: customers who switched from them, with permission.
- **Last updated** date and sources.

## How to talk about competitors
- Never bash. Acknowledge, then reframe on the buyer's priorities.
  > "They're a solid choice for {use case}. Teams that pick us usually care most about {our strength}. How important is that for you?"
- Win on fit for this buyer, not on feature lists.

## Maintenance
Review quarterly, and whenever a deal is lost to that competitor (feed from `sales-win-loss`).
