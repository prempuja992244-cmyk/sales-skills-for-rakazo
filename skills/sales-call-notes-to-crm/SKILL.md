# Call notes to CRM

## Extract
From the notes/transcript, pull:
- Attendees and roles
- Pains (quote their words), impact, priority
- Current solution and competitors mentioned
- Budget signals, timeline, critical event
- Decision makers and process
- Objections raised and how they were left
- Agreed next steps: what, who, by when
- Buying signals and risks

## Summary format (paste into CRM note)
```
Meeting: {date} — {company} — {type}
Who: ...
Their situation: ...
Pains: ...
Impact / why now: ...
Decision process: ...
Objections: ...
Next steps: [ ] task — owner — due date
Deal risk: Low/Med/High — reason
```

## CRM updates
Propose field changes before writing: stage, amount, close date, next step, next step date, contact roles, competitor, lead source. Use the connected CRM app (HubSpot, Zoho, Pipedrive, Salesforce, Google Sheets). Show a before → after table; write only after approval.

## Tasks and follow-up
- Create tasks for each next step with due dates.
- Draft the recap email (`sales-follow-up`, "After a meeting").

## Rules
- Separate facts said by the prospect from your interpretation.
- Don't move a deal to a later stage without the exit criteria for that stage (see `sales-pipeline-review`).
- Don't store sensitive personal data that isn't needed for the sale.
