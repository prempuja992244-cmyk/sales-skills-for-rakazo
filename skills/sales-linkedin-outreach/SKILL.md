# LinkedIn outreach and social selling

## 1. Seller profile (check once)
- Headline says who you help and the outcome, not just job title.
- About section: problem → how you solve it → proof → call to action.
- Featured: one case study or demo link.

## 2. Warm up before asking
For each target: view profile, follow the company, react or leave a thoughtful comment on a recent post (adds a point of view, not "Great post!").

## 3. Connection request (≤ 200 characters, no pitch)
Reference something real: shared group, event, post, mutual connection, or their company news.
> "Hi {first_name}, saw your post on {topic} — the point about {detail} matched what we see with {industry} teams. Would be good to connect."

## 4. After acceptance
- Message 1 (wait 1–2 days): thank + one question about their world. No pitch.
- Message 2 (if they reply): share a relevant insight or resource; ask if the problem is a priority.
- Message 3: offer a short call only when there's interest.
- If no reply after 2 messages, stop and keep engaging with their content.

## 5. Inbound through content (weekly)
Suggest 2–3 posts per week: a customer story, a lesson/mistake, a practical how-to, a strong opinion about the industry. Draft them for the user's approval.

## Automation limits
- Use LinkedIn only through the user's own logged-in session on the bot's computer, at human pace (e.g. ≤ 20 connection requests and ≤ 50 messages per day).
- Do not use scraping tools that break LinkedIn's terms; do not log in with someone else's account.
- Every message is a draft until the user approves it.
