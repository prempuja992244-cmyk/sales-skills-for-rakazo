# Cold email

## Before writing
- Read `sales-context.md` and the lead card (`sales-lead-research`). If there is no hook, research first or ask the user.
- Pick one goal for the email: a reply, a short call, or permission to send something. Never more than one ask.

## Structure (under 120 words, ideally 50–90)
1. **Subject**: 2–5 words, lowercase feel, specific to them. No clickbait, no "Quick question" spam patterns. Examples: `new pune branch`, `invoice delays at acme`.
2. **Opening line**: a true, specific observation about them (trigger event, post, product, job opening). Not about us.
3. **Problem**: the pain that observation usually causes, in their words.
4. **Value**: one sentence on the outcome we deliver + one proof point (customer, number) from the context file.
5. **Ask**: low-friction, interest-based question. E.g. "Worth a 15-minute look?" / "Should I send a 2-minute video?"
6. **Signature**: name, role, company, one link max.

## Quality checks (fix any that fail)
- Remove the first line: does the email still make sense? If yes, personalisation is too weak — rewrite it.
- Count "I/we" vs "you". "You" should win.
- No attachments, no more than one link, no images in the first touch.
- No spam words in excess (free!!!, guaranteed, act now), no ALL CAPS.
- Reads naturally aloud; matches the tone and language in `sales-context.md` (English, Hindi or Hinglish).
- Every claim is backed by the context file. Never invent results or customers.

## Variants
Offer 2 variants with different angles (pain-led vs. proof-led) so the user can A/B test (`sales-ab-testing`).

## Sending
- Draft only. Show drafts to the user and get approval before sending from a connected mailbox.
- Include an easy opt-out line where required (see `sales-outreach-compliance`).
- Log the sent email in the CRM/sheet with date and variant.
