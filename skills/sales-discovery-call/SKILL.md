# Discovery call

## Before the call (prep brief, 1 page)
1. Who: attendees, roles, LinkedIn summaries (`sales-lead-research`).
2. Company: what they do, recent news, likely priorities.
3. Hypothesis: the 2 most likely pains and why.
4. Goal for this call: e.g. confirm pain + agree to a demo with decision maker.
5. Agenda to send in advance (3 bullets, 30 minutes).

## Call structure (30 min)
- 0–3 min: rapport, confirm time and agenda, ask what they want from the call.
- 3–20 min: discovery questions (below). They should talk ~70%.
- 20–25 min: share a relevant story/proof tied to what they said.
- 25–30 min: agree next step with a date in the calendar.

## Question bank
**Situation**: How do you handle {process} today? Who's involved? What tools?
**Problem**: What's hardest about that? How often does it go wrong?
**Implication**: What does that cost you — time, money, customers? Who else feels it?
**Need-payoff**: If that were fixed, what would change? How would you measure it?
**Critical event**: Is there a date that makes this urgent?
**Decision**: Who else weighs in? How have you bought similar things before? What would stop this?
**Budget**: Is there a budget set aside, or would we need to build the case?

## After the call
- Summarise in their words: pains, impact, critical event, decision process, open questions.
- Fill qualification (`sales-lead-scoring` BANT/MEDDIC) — mark unknowns.
- Send recap email within 24 hours (`sales-follow-up`) and update the CRM (`sales-call-notes-to-crm`).

## Analysing notes or a transcript
Highlight confirmed needs vs. assumptions, talk ratio if a transcript exists, missed questions, and the strongest next step.
