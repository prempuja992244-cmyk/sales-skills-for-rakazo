# Closing

## Buying signals to act on
Questions about implementation, start dates, contracts, payment terms, team training; bringing new stakeholders; asking for references; negotiating details.

## Trial closes (check readiness without pressure)
- "Based on what you've seen, does this solve {pain}?"
- "If we started on the 1st, would that work for your team?"
- "What would you need to see to move ahead?"

## Mutual action plan (for deals with 2+ steps left)
Write a shared plan with dates and owners on both sides:
| Step | Owner | Date |
|---|---|---|
| Final demo for finance head | Us + {champion} | ... |
| Security / legal review | {their IT/legal} | ... |
| Contract sent | Us | ... |
| Signature | {decision maker} | ... |
| Kickoff | Both | ... |
Work back from their go-live date. Share it and review it in every follow-up.

## Asking for the business
Be direct once value is confirmed:
> "Everything we discussed points to starting with {option}. Shall I send the agreement today so we can begin on {date}?"

## After yes
1. Send agreement/invoice/payment link the same day (after user approval).
2. Confirm kickoff date and owners.
3. Update CRM to `Won`, record win reason (`sales-win-loss`).
4. Thank them; ask for a referral after first success (`sales-upsell-referrals`).

## Stalled at the end
Find the blocker: unknown decision maker, budget timing, risk fear, competing priority. Address that specific blocker; offer a pilot or phased start if risk is the issue. Use `sales-follow-up` ghost template if silent.
