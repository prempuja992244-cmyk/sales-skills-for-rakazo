# WhatsApp and SMS selling

## Who you may message
- **Allowed**: people who messaged you first, existing customers, and leads who gave clear opt-in (form checkbox, "WhatsApp me" click, written consent).
- **Not allowed**: bought or scraped number lists, cold blasting strangers. This gets numbers banned and may break telecom/marketing rules (e.g. TRAI DND/commercial communication rules in India).

## Enquiry-to-sale flow
1. **Reply fast** (under 5 minutes in business hours). Greet by name, confirm what they asked.
2. **Qualify in 2–3 questions**: need, quantity/size, timeline, location, budget band.
3. **Send the right thing**: catalog item, price, photo/video, or a 1-page PDF — one thing at a time.
4. **Make the next step easy**: book a call, visit, payment link, or order confirmation.
5. **Log** the lead in the CRM/sheet with source `WhatsApp`.

## Follow-up rhythm for open enquiries
- Same day: answer any questions, share proof (review, photo of past work).
- Day 2: one helpful nudge ("Any question on sizes/delivery?").
- Day 5: last useful offer or alternative option.
- Then stop; move to monthly broadcast only if they opted in.

## Message style
- Short (1–3 lines), friendly, in the customer's language (Hindi/Hinglish/English as they write).
- Voice notes only if the customer uses them.
- Business templates for messages sent after the 24-hour customer-service window (WhatsApp Business API).

## Automation in Rakazo
- Connect WhatsApp Business through an integration if available; otherwise draft replies for the user to send.
- Draft only; every outgoing message needs user approval unless auto-replies were explicitly approved for FAQ answers.
- Stop immediately when someone says stop/unsubscribe/"mat bhejo" and record the opt-out.
