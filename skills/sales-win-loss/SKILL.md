# Win-loss analysis

## Record every closed deal
Win/loss | Amount | Cycle length | Segment | Competitor | Primary reason | Secondary reason | Stage where it was lost | Quote from buyer (if any).

Reason categories:
- **Loss**: price, chose competitor, no decision/status quo, timing/budget, missing feature, poor fit, lost champion, slow follow-up, trust/risk.
- **Win**: pain severity, speed, proof/case study, relationship, price/value, feature, service.

## Collect the real reason
For lost deals, a short, respectful message:
> "Thanks for considering us. To get better, could you share the main reason you went another way? One line is plenty."

## Analyse (monthly or every 20 closed deals)
1. Win rate overall and by segment, source, deal size, competitor, and sales owner.
2. Top 3 loss reasons and the stage they happen in.
3. Patterns in wins: what was true in most wins (e.g. demo within 7 days, champion present).
4. Cycle length: where deals wait longest.

## Output
- 5 findings, each with evidence (numbers + example deals).
- 3 recommended changes, each tied to a skill: targeting (`sales-icp-builder`), messaging (`sales-cold-email`), objections (`sales-objection-handling`), competitor (`sales-battlecard`), process (`sales-pipeline-review`).

## Rules
Small samples are noisy — say so when fewer than ~20 deals. Don't blame individuals; focus on process.
