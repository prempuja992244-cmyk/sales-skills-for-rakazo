# Trigger events and buying signals

## Signals to watch (strongest first)
1. New leader in the buyer role (first 90 days = high openness to change).
2. Funding round, acquisition, or big contract win.
3. Job posts for roles our product helps or replaces.
4. New office, branch, store, or market entry.
5. Product launch or rebrand.
6. Regulation or deadline affecting them (GST changes, data protection rules, audits).
7. Public complaints about their current vendor or tool.
8. They engaged with us: replied, booked, visited pricing, attended a webinar.

## Routine (good for a weekly Rakazo routine)
1. Load the list of named target accounts (CRM view, sheet, or `target-accounts.md`).
2. For each account, search news, the company's site/blog, LinkedIn company page, and job boards for the last 7–30 days.
3. Record each new signal: Account | Signal | Date | Source link | Strength (High/Med/Low).
4. For High signals, draft a 2–3 sentence outreach that names the event and links it to a relevant outcome. Use `sales-cold-email` or `sales-linkedin-outreach` for the format.
5. Post a digest to the user: top 5 accounts to act on this week with drafts attached. Do not send anything without approval.

## Rules
- Only cite events with a source link and date; skip rumours.
- Congratulate or reference the event naturally; never imply you were tracking them closely.
- If no signals were found, say so plainly rather than stretching weak ones.
