# Proposal and quotation writer

## Inputs
- Discovery notes / CRM summary (pains, impact, decision process, timeline).
- `sales-context.md` for offer, pricing rules, proof, terms.
- Which format: email proposal, PDF/Doc, slide deck, or simple quotation.

## Proposal structure
1. **Title**: `{Outcome} for {Company}`.
2. **Their situation** (in their words): 3–5 bullets from discovery.
3. **What success looks like**: measurable goals and the date that matters.
4. **Recommended solution**: only what solves stated needs. Map each item to a pain.
5. **Plan and timeline**: phases, owners on both sides, milestones.
6. **Investment**: 2–3 options (good/better/best) when allowed; show what each includes. Taxes (e.g. GST) and payment terms stated clearly.
7. **Proof**: one relevant case study or testimonial.
8. **Why us / risks handled**: guarantees, support, pilot.
9. **Next steps**: sign/accept by date, kickoff date, contact.

## Quotation (short format)
Customer details · Quote number and date · Validity period · Line items (item, qty, unit price, amount) · Subtotal · Discount · Tax · Total · Payment terms · Delivery timeline · Terms and conditions · Acceptance line.

## ROI / business case (one page)
Cost of problem today (time × rate, lost revenue, errors) vs. cost of solution → payback period. Show the assumptions and let the buyer change them.

## Rules
- Use only approved prices, discounts and terms. Mark anything unapproved as `[NEEDS APPROVAL]`.
- Keep it short: buyers skim. 2–5 pages for most deals.
- Produce the document with the bot's document tools; send to the prospect only after user approval, then schedule a follow-up for day 3.
