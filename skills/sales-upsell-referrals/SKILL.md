# Upsell, renewals and referrals

## Timing (ask after value, not after payment)
- **Day 7–14**: onboarding check-in — are they getting the promised result? Fix issues first.
- **First win**: ask for a review/testimonial and a referral.
- **Usage or growth signal** (more users, new branch, higher volume): suggest the next plan or add-on.
- **60 days before renewal**: value review with results achieved, then renewal proposal.

## Find expansion opportunities
From CRM, usage data, and conversations, flag customers who: hit plan limits, mentioned another team's pain, opened a new location, hired in a related area, or praised the product. Rank by potential value.

## Messages
**Referral ask**
> "Glad {result} is working for you. Is there one other {role} you know who deals with {pain}? Happy to take great care of them — and I'll mention it was you only if you'd like."

**Review ask**
> Short thank-you + direct link to the review page + 2 prompt questions (what problem, what changed).

**Upsell**
> Lead with the observed signal and the outcome, offer a quick call; never surprise them with price in the first message.

## Rules
Only use testimonials and customer names with written permission. Referral rewards only as defined in `sales-context.md`.
